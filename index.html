Our story, from the beginning till now ❤️

I don't even know where to begin, because I never imagined that a few random conversations, shared reels, and little moments would turn into something this special.

It all started so casually. We began talking, sharing things, teasing each other, and slowly became a part of each other's everyday lives. Somewhere between those random conversations, silly arguments, endless teasing, and little acts of care, you became someone I started looking forward to every single day.

Then came those little hints, the butterflies, and the questions we were both probably too shy to ask directly. And eventually, we admitted what our hearts had been trying to say all along — that this wasn't just friendship anymore. The day you told me you had fallen in love with me too became one of my favourite moments. 🥹❤️

Since then, every conversation has meant a little more. Your texts bring a smile to my face, your happiness matters to me, and even our smallest moments have become memories I want to keep forever. I love teasing you, making you smile, caring for you, and being someone you can talk to without having to pretend to be okay.

I know we aren't perfect, and we don't have to be. There will be silly fights, mood swings, misunderstandings, and countless moments of laughter. But I hope we keep choosing to understand each other, grow together, and make many more beautiful memories.

And now, here we are — you, me, and a story that's still being written. I don't know exactly what every tomorrow will look like, but I know I'm really happy that our paths crossed.

Happy Birthday, my favourite girl. Thank you for becoming such a beautiful part of my life. This is just the beginning of our story, and I can't wait for all the chapters still waiting for us.

I love youuu, meri jaan. ♾️❤️
